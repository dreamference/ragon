# Ragon commercial licence: pricing

> **Not for publication before the entity.** Private until the new Dreamference legal entity exists. Nothing is sold, quoted or promised before then. The public form is the site draft, `website/src/drafts/pricing.md`, which goes live with the website before 1.0, once the entity exists.
>
> - **Status:** owner decisions of 2026-11-14 (Q255-Q266), written down as decided. Background and comparables: the pricing research report of 2026-11-12.
> - **Not legal advice.** The licence text (grant conditions, audit, governing law, VAT) needs counsel for the entity.

## 1. Basis

- Ragon is AGPL-3.0-or-later for everyone, free, with no exceptions (Q229). The commercial licence is the way out of the AGPL for those who cannot or will not meet its terms (section 13 for network use, closed embedding, a blanket "no AGPL" policy).
- The **licensor** is the new Dreamference entity, which also holds the CLA (Q144, Q228). Nothing is sold before it exists.
- **Currency:** list prices are in **USD**, excluding tax. EUR is shown as indicative only and does not bind.
- **Unit: one node.** A node is one machine serving one model. GB10-class, Mac and Windows machines count alike. The number of developers or users per node does not matter.
- **Size proxy:** annual group revenue, USD-equivalent, from the filed accounts of the last closed fiscal year.

## 2. Tiers

All prices are per node per year, list, excluding tax.

| Tier | Who (annual group revenue) | Price per node per year | Node cap at this price |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0 Free | individuals, non-profits, education, and companies under 1 M USD-equivalent revenue **and** under 2 M funding | 0 (registration, no support) | 3 nodes |
| 1 Small | 1 M to 10 M | 300 USD | per-tier cap (2.1) |
| 2 Mid | 10 M to 100 M | 1,200 USD | per-tier cap (2.1) |
| 3 Large | over 100 M | 3,600 USD, minimum 20,000 USD a year | per-tier cap (2.1) |

### 2.1 Caps and discounts

- Every tier has a node cap at its price. Nodes above the cap are priced at the next tier's rate (so a 3 M company cannot run 300 nodes at the small-tier price). The free tier above 3 nodes pays tier 1 for the extra nodes. The cap sizes are set in the order forms of the entity; the research proposal was 10 / 50 / none for tiers 1 / 2 / 3.
- **Volume discounts** start at **10 nodes** and **25 nodes**. The research proposal: 10 % from 10 nodes (tier 2), 20 % from 25 nodes (tier 3). The percentages are not part of the owner's decision and are confirmed with the entity's order form.
- **Floor:** the large tier costs at least **20,000 USD a year**, whatever the node count.
- **Enterprise agreement** above **100 nodes**: negotiated, not list.

## 3. Hosting, OEM, redistribution

Hosting for third parties, OEM and redistribution (the cases where the AGPL bites) are priced at the **mid-tier price at minimum**, whatever the company's own size, because the value comes from the customers served. They are not offered at the free or small-tier price.

## 4. Support (bundled per tier)

| Tier | E-mail support |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | first reply within 5 business days |
| 2 | first reply within 1 business day; Severity 1 within 4 hours |
| 3 | named contact; Severity 1 within 1 hour |

- The free tier has no support and no SLA.
- **24x7 support** is an add-on at **+20 %** of the licence fee.
- Support belongs to the paid tiers' order forms; it is not promised for the AGPL use.

## 5. Evaluation

**90 days**, free, any company size, non-production, **once per organisation**, under the commercial terms.

## 6. Verification

- **Self-declaration** of tier and node count in the order form, at order and at each renewal, signed by an officer of the customer.
- **One audit a year**, with notice, by an independent auditor under NDA.
- **True-up at renewal:** the tier changes at the next renewal, on the last closed fiscal year. A threshold crossed mid-term does not trigger a retroactive charge. **No retroactive charges.**
- **Quarterly node reports** for tiers 2 and 3.
- Exchange rate for revenue in other currencies: the ECB rate on the last day of the fiscal year.

## 7. Open for the entity
