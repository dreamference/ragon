# Pricing

Ragon is open source under the AGPL-3.0-or-later and free to use. The commercial licence is for organisations that cannot or will not meet the AGPL's terms: hosting Ragon for others, embedding it in a closed product, or a company policy that forbids AGPL software. It is sold by Dreamference.

Prices are per node per year in US dollars, excluding tax. EUR amounts shown are indicative. A node is one machine serving one model: a GB10-class machine, a Mac or a Windows PC alike. As many people as you like can use a node.

| | Free | Small | Mid | Large |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Annual revenue | under 1 M USD and under 2 M funding; individuals, non-profits, education | 1 M to 10 M | 10 M to 100 M | over 100 M |
| Per node per year | 0 | 300 USD | 1,200 USD | 3,600 USD |
| Nodes | up to 3 | tier cap | tier cap | tier cap |
| Support | none | e-mail, first reply in 5 business days | e-mail, 1 business day, Severity 1 in 4 hours | named contact, Severity 1 in 1 hour |

- Volume discounts from 10 and from 25 nodes.
- The Large tier costs at least 20,000 USD a year. Above 100 nodes we agree an enterprise agreement.
- 24x7 support is available for +20 % of the licence fee.
- Hosting for third parties, OEM and redistribution start at the Mid price, whatever your size.
- Free 90-day evaluation for any company, for non-production use, once per organisation.

## How the tier is set

Your tier follows the annual revenue of your group in the last closed fiscal year. You declare it when you order and at each renewal; we may audit once a year. A tier change takes effect at the next renewal, with no retroactive charge. Tiers Mid and Large report their node count every quarter.
